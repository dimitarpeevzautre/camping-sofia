We're still building the real camp.

Until it opens, the guests keep coming to this one. Drive each of them to the pitch that lights up. Park square and quickly for points; clean parkings in a row multiply them. Bumps cost you and bring the warden after you; three guests who give up waiting end your shift.

What is on the roads
  • Yellow coin: 25 points. Blue: 8 more seconds.
  • Purple token: a boost. Speed, time freeze, double points, self-parking, a shield or a tip rush.
  • Green marker: an errand. Pick it up and take it to the pitch for 120 points.
  • The dog with the sausages: catch up with it for 200 points.
  • Red stars: the warden is after you. A fine hurts; a clean parking calms him down.
  • The ice-cream van is slow and in no hurry. Go round it.
  • Cones fly when you hit them: 5 points each, but the warden notices.
  • The three clubs remember you: park their members cleanly and they pay more.
  • Ten garden gnomes are hidden around the camp. They are worth finding.
  • Parking in the rain or after dark pays more, but the road is slippery.
Camp shop:

Every point you score becomes a coin when the shift ends.

    Arrow keys or WASD to drive. Space is the handbrake: at speed it swings the back round. H honks, M mutes, R tunes the radio, P pauses. A gamepad works too.
